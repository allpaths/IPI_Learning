# IRPhoton: A Proposed Test of M/E/I

<div class="ipi-hero">
  <p class="ipi-hero-subtitle">
    Study companions to a design study for detecting the infrared
    "information energy photons" that Dr.&nbsp;Melvin&nbsp;Vopson's
    mass–energy–information equivalence principle predicts from
    electron–positron annihilation.
  </p>
  <span class="ipi-hero-tag">manuscript in preparation · not peer reviewed · 9 companions · about 143 min</span>
</div>

In 2022 Dr. Vopson proposed a direct test of the M/E/I principle: if a
particle's information has an energy equivalent, erasing it by annihilation
should release that energy as infrared photons whose wavelength depends on the
temperature of the target. The manuscript *Design of an experiment to detect
information energy photons from electron–positron annihilation* (Jeff Dillon)
turns that proposal into a concrete experimental design and estimates the
signal, the backgrounds and the time needed for a detection. The pages below
walk through it section by section, with the real output of the scripts that
produce every number.
