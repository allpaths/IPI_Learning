Recommended reading order#

The companions follow the manuscript section by section. Each one quotes the paper paragraph by paragraph, defines every symbol and acronym, pastes the real output of the scripts that produced its numbers, separates what is assumed from what is established, and ends with referee questions and self-check questions. Reading times are estimates (about 200 words per minute plus time for code cells and questions).

Read 02, 04 and the "Worked numbers" and "Assumed vs established" parts of 06. That covers the argument and every headline number in about 40 minutes.

Where the numbers come from#

Every number in the paper is printed by a script in scripts/: compute_prediction.py (Section II), and in scripts/simulation/: ir_escape.py, ps_formation.py, sensitivity.py, background_estimates.py. Their outputs are saved in scripts/simulation/outputs/. Numbers from the Geant4 model (collected fraction, stopping depths, gamma response) were not re-run for this paper; the companions flag them wherever they appear.
